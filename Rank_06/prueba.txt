PRUEBA

Con tres terminales:

Terminal 1 — servidor

./mini_serv 8080

Terminal 2 — cliente 0

nc 127.0.0.1 8080

Terminal 3 — cliente 1

nc 127.0.0.1 8080

Y comprobar:

client 0 conecta
        ↓
client 1 conecta
        ↓
client 0 recibe:
server: client 1 just arrived

client 0 escribe:
hola

client 1 recibe:
client 0: hola

client 1 escribe:
adios

client 0 recibe:
client 1: adios

client 1 se desconecta

client 0 recibe:
server: client 1 just left
